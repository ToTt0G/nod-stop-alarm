Doze off. Nod wakes you before your stop.

Nod is a free, open-source stop alarm for train, bus, tram, metro and ferry rides. Pick your destination, close your eyes, and Nod wakes you a few minutes before you arrive. It's a GPS location alarm built for one job: making sure you never miss your stop again.

PICK YOUR STOP
- Search any station, stop or city worldwide (OpenStreetMap data).
- Or drop a pin on the map and drag the zone from 100 m to 20 km.
- Saved places and history let you start a trip in one tap.

WAKES YOU RIGHT ON TIME
- Wake on entering the zone, or a set number of minutes before arrival based on your average speed while moving.
- Alarm, vibration only, or silent. The volume ramps up gently.
- A soft heads-up about 2 minutes before the real alarm.
- A full-screen alarm over the lock screen with slide to dismiss.

LIVE ON YOUR LOCK SCREEN
- On Android 16, the Live Update shows your distance and ETA on the lock screen and always-on display, with a status-bar chip and an End trip button.
- On older Android versions, a regular ongoing notification keeps you informed.

HEADPHONES IN? NO PROBLEM
- The alarm plays only in your ears and your music pauses.
- A spoken heads-up plays over your music.
- Press the earbud button to say "I'm awake".

WIDGETS AND SHORTCUTS
- Journey, Ring and Quick stop widgets update live during a trip, also on the lock screen where supported.
- A Quick Settings tile and launcher shortcuts for your favourite stops.

ZERO BATTERY WHEN IDLE
- No trip set means no background services, alarms, jobs or location use.
- During a trip, Nod checks your location adaptively: every few minutes via Wi-Fi/cell positioning when you're far away, then GPS only in the final stretch.
- Widgets render with no background jobs.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
- No account, no ads, no tracking, no analytics.
- Your location never leaves your phone.
- The only network use is place search and map tiles.
- Open source under the MIT license.

Nod works for commuters who nap on the train, night bus riders, long-distance travellers and anyone who gets lost in a book or a podcast. Set a destination alarm once and relax until your stop.

Available in English and Swedish. Requires Android 8.0 or later. Lock-screen Live Updates need Android 16.
