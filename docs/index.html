Free, open-source stop alarm for Android

Doze off. Nod wakes you before your stop.

A GPS location alarm for trains, buses, trams, metros and ferries. Pick your stop, close your eyes, and Nod wakes you a few minutes before you arrive. Zero battery when idle, and your location never leaves the phone.

Set it and sleep

One search, one tap, and you're covered for the whole ride. Nod does one thing well and then gets out of the way.

Pick your stop

Search any station, stop or city worldwide, powered by OpenStreetMap. Or drop a pin and drag the zone from 100 m to 20 km.

Wake by distance or time

Ring on entering the zone, or N minutes before arrival calculated from your live speed. A soft heads-up arrives about two minutes early.

Wakes you your way

Alarm, vibration only, or silent. The volume ramps up gently, and the full-screen lock-screen alarm dismisses with a slide.

Live on your lock screen

On Android 16, the Live Update shows distance and ETA on the lock screen and always-on display, with a status-bar chip and an End trip button. Older Android gets a regular ongoing notification.

Headphone-aware

With headphones in, the alarm plays only in your ears and your music pauses. A spoken heads-up plays over your music, and the earbud button means "I'm awake".

Widgets and shortcuts

Journey, Ring and Quick stop widgets update live during a trip, also on the lock screen where supported. Plus a Quick Settings tile and launcher shortcuts.

Light on battery, silent on data

Most location alarms keep GPS awake for the whole ride and phone home with your position. Nod does neither.

Questions

Is there an app that wakes me up at my bus stop?

Yes. Nod is a free Android stop alarm. Search your bus stop or station, start the trip, and Nod wakes you when you enter the zone around the stop or a set number of minutes before you arrive. It works for buses, trains, trams, metros and ferries.

How does a train stop alarm work?

Nod checks your location during the trip and compares it with the stop you picked. Far from the stop it checks every few minutes using Wi-Fi and cell positioning; in the final stretch it switches to GPS. When you enter the zone, or when your live speed says you are N minutes away, it rings a full-screen alarm over the lock screen, with a soft heads-up about two minutes earlier.

Is Nod a GPS destination alarm for any location, not just stations?

Yes. You can search any station, stop or city worldwide, or drop a pin anywhere on the map and drag the alarm zone from 100 m to 20 km. It works as a general location alarm for Android.

Will I still wake up if I fall asleep on the train with headphones in?

Yes. With headphones connected, the alarm plays only in your ears and your music pauses. A spoken heads-up plays over your music before the alarm, and pressing the earbud button tells Nod you are awake.

Does a location alarm drain the battery?

Nod uses zero battery when no trip is set: no background services, alarms, jobs or location. During a trip it checks location adaptively, every few minutes via Wi-Fi and cell when far away, and GPS only in the final stretch. Widgets render with no background jobs.

Does Nod track me or need an account?

No. There is no account, no ads, no tracking and no analytics. Your location never leaves the phone. The only network use is place search (Photon, OpenStreetMap data) and map tiles (OpenFreeMap). The code is open source under the MIT license.

Finns det en väckarklocka för hållplatsen på Android?

Ja. Nod är ett gratis hållplatsalarm med öppen källkod för Android. Sök din station eller hållplats, starta resan och somna. Nod väcker dig när du kommer in i zonen eller ett valt antal minuter före ankomst. Appen finns på svenska och engelska, använder noll batteri när ingen resa är satt och skickar aldrig din position någonstans.
